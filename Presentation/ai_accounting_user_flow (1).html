AI Accounting User Flow — Financial Close Experience

User-centric flow showing how accountants, reviewers, controllers, and executives interact with NoovaERP during financial close using AI. This version includes AI-OCR to assist preparation from manual documents and manual input, while keeping canonical accounting truth, reconciliation truth, and close authorization under controlled services.

Legend — Flow Types

User Action
What the accountant, reviewer, controller, or CFO actively does in the ERP.
AI Function
What AI extracts, detects, explains, scores, suggests, or forecasts.
System / Workspace
What the close workspace and orchestration engine manage automatically.
Human Decision
Points where users accept, edit, reject, approve, return, or escalate.
Control / Canonical Gate
Policy, entitlement, SoD, audit, and final canonical close authority.

Entity / Book

  • Book = accounting ledger/book scope
Important design rule: AI-OCR and other AI capabilities may assist data extraction, classification, explanation, and draft suggestion, but they must not become the truth owner. Final accounting truth, reconciliation truth, and period close authority remain canonical and controlled.
