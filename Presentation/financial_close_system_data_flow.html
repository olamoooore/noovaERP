Financial Close System + Data Application Flow

System-centric application flow derived from the AI accounting user journey. This view shows how user actions move through UI, application services, AI services, policy gates, events, and canonical data stores during financial close. It also includes AI-OCR preparation support for manual source documents.

Design rule: AI-OCR and Financial Close AI may enrich draft and review workflows, but they must not own accounting truth, reconciliation truth, or final period close authority. The application layer may orchestrate, validate, and explain, while canonical services remain the only truth-mutating and close-authorizing boundary.
